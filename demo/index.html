Canvas applications

Build something with F5

Three small applications that turn canvas drawing, reusable geometry, and animation into useful interactions.

01 / DRAW & EXPORT

Image annotation

Mark up a workspace concept or your own image. Draw arrows, rectangles, and freehand notes, then download the result.

  • Mouse, pen, and touch drawing
  • Undo and PNG export
  • Local image upload
Annotate an image
02 / SELECT & INSPECT

Seating plan

Explore a restaurant floor plan. Select a table, inspect its capacity, and try a reservation without leaving the canvas.

  • Shape-based hit testing
  • Availability filtering
  • Keyboard-accessible selection
Explore the floor plan
03 / ANIMATE & FOLLOW

Delivery map

Follow three simulated couriers around a neighborhood. Inspect their routes, pause the action, or change playback speed.

  • Time-based movement
  • Vectors and reusable paths
  • Reduced-motion support
Follow the deliveries
